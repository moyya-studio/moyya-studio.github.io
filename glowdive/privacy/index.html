GLOWDIVE

Privacy Policy

Developer: Moyya Studio · Contact: moyya.studio@gmail.com · Effective: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Glowdive ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by Moyya Studio ("we"). There are no accounts: we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (best depth, coins, gear, your aquarium and decorations, the Fish Book, settings and which tips you have seen) is saved on your device. The game also stores your age group (under 13, or 13 and over) and when you answered the age question. The birth year you pick is used only to work out that group: it is not stored, logged or sent anywhere.

2. The age question

The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year. This lets us show the right kind of ads. If you are under 13, every ad the game requests is marked as directed at children, is not personalised and is limited to general-audience content, no ad consent form is shown, and analytics do not use advertising identifiers.

Some features work through Google services. These services process information under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Game settings file

When it starts, the game downloads a small settings file from this website (moyya-studio.github.io) so we can adjust balance, such as rewards and how often ads appear, without an update. The download sends no information about you. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may record the request's IP address in its server logs.

5. Reminders

If you agree, the game sets one reminder on your phone when your aquarium's visitor coins are full, at most once a day and never at night. Reminders are created and shown on your device only; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your Android notification settings.

6. Ads and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland and 13 or over, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button on the game's start screen (Change ad choices). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings. Buying No Ads removes the ads between dives; rewarded ads stay optional.

7. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the game: to save your progress, to deliver your purchase, to show ads, and to fix problems and balance the game. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for anything else.

8. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. Because children may still play it, it asks every player's birth year and gives players under 13 only child-safe, non-personalised ads, without advertising identifiers. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Purchases made with real money are kept by Google Play so you can restore them. To ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your copy of the game, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

Information sent to Google services and the settings file download use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we only use well-established services and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Effective" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
